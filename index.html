I’m a Senior AI Developer at Flux Fortify, where I build the automation and the platforms behind it — AI pipelines that read documents and drive back-office systems, and the web and mobile products clients actually put in front of their customers.

Most of my work now sits at the seam between an LLM and a real business process: parsing a signed contract, matching it against a live API, branching on state-specific rules, and writing the result back with an audit trail. The interesting part is never the model — it’s everything around it that has to not break.

Before that I spent a summer in market research at Stratview Research, running primary interviews with aerospace and OEM experts. It taught me to check a claim before repeating it, which turns out to be the same instinct good engineering needs.
